Character creation in Ressam builds your character through deliberate choices, not random rolls or class restrictions. Every decision shapes who your character is and what they can do.

**What You'll Need:**

- A character sheet ([Basic Character Creator](https://levianeer.github.io/Ressam-TTRPG/templates/character/character_sheet.html))
- This rules (Core Rules, Combat, and Equipment chapters)
- A concept in mind (or willingness to discover one)

## Step 1: Starting Experience

Set **EXP to 30** (Level 1). XP required for your next level is **(Current Level \+ Next Level) × 30** \- each level costs more than the last. See the **Progression & Rewards** chapter for the full advancement table.

---

## Step 2: Assign Priorities

Take the five priorities, **A** through **E**, and assign one to each of the five categories below - no repeats, all five used. This single choice shapes your character before you've spent a single point: where your **A** goes is what you're actually good at, and your **E** is what you're openly trading away for it.

| Priority | Attributes (Array) | Skills (Pool) | Career             | Feats | Race                            |
|:--------:|:------------------:|:-------------:|:-------------------|:-----:|:--------------------------------|
|  **A**   |     3, 2, 1, 0     |      18       | Any Status tier    |   4   | Any Race Tier                   |
|  **B**   |     2, 2, 1, 0     |      15       | Up to Upper-Middle |   3   | Up to Tier B (Uncanny)          |
|  **C**   |     2, 1, 1, 0     |      12       | Up to Middle       |   2   | Up to Tier C (Exotic)           |
|  **D**   |     1, 1, 1, 0     |       9       | Up to Lower-Middle |   1   | Up to Tier D (Marked)           |
|  **E**   |     1, 1, 0, 0     |       6       | Low only           |   0   | Tier E only (the four Artefolk) |

One number per Attribute (STR/DEX/MIND/CHA): only an **A** ever reaches a 3, and every tier but **E** carries exactly one guaranteed 0 (**E** carries two - the tier that's openly trading Attributes away for everything else).

- **Attributes** hands you a **standard array** for Step 5 - six fixed numbers, one per Attribute, assigned in whatever order you like. There is no Attribute point pool and nothing to buy: a higher priority is a better set of numbers, not more currency. Only an **A** starts with a 3 in anything.
- **Skills** sets the size of your Skill point budget for Step 5 - those points are still spent freely within the normal caps, this only changes how many you have.
- **Career** sets the highest Status tier you may choose from in Step 4 - "up to" a tier means that tier and everything below it is open (a **B** can still take a Low Status career if the concept calls for it, it just isn't locked out of anything above Low the way a **D** or **E** is).
- **Feats** sets how many Feats you start with in Step 6.
- **Race** sets which Race Tier(s) you may choose from in Step 3 - "up to" a tier means that tier and everything below it is open, same as Career (see [[Races|races_overview]]). There's no cost beyond the gate itself - once your priority opens a tier, every race in it is free to take.

## Step 3: Choose Race

See the **Races** chapter, limited to what your Race priority allows (Step 2). Record:

- Racial abilities
- Movement speed
- Special senses (darkvision, low-light vision)

## Step 4: Choose Career

See the **Careers** chapter, limited to the Status tier your Career priority allows (Step 2). Record:

- Starting equipment
- Starting currency

## Step 5: Assign Attributes, Distribute Skill Points

Your two Step 2 priorities pay out in two different currencies here, and neither converts into the other.

### **Attributes: assign the array**

Your Attributes priority hands you a **standard array** \- four fixed numbers. **Assign one to each of your four Attributes** (STR, DEX, MIND, CHA), in whatever order you like. There is nothing to buy and no budget to run out of: a better priority is a better set of numbers, not a bigger pool.

| Priority | Standard Array |
|:--------:|:----------------|
|  **A**   | 3, 2, 1, 0       |
|  **B**   | 2, 2, 1, 0       |
|  **C**   | 2, 1, 1, 0       |
|  **D**   | 1, 1, 1, 0       |
|  **E**   | 1, 1, 0, 0       |

**Every array is used whole.** You take all four numbers, the zeroes included \- you can't decline one, trade two in for a higher one, or leave a number unassigned. **A 3 is a Level 1 ceiling only an A reaches**, and only in a single Attribute.

**Racial modifiers (Step 3) are applied afterward, on top of the array**, and aren't limited by it: a race with \+2 in an Attribute you gave your 3 starts you at 5, and a race with \-1 in one you gave a 0 starts you at \-1. Both are legal (see Attribute Limits, below).

### **Skills: spend the pool**

Your Skills priority is a real point budget, spent one point per Skill Rank. Career skill bonuses are free, applied first, and don't count against it. One cap shapes how far any single Skill can go:

- **A Skill's Rank can never exceed its governing Attribute's current score** (see [[Skills|core_rules]]) \- want Two-Handed Blades 3? STR needs to be 3 first. An Attribute you assigned a 0 or lower locks every Skill under it at 0, which is the real price of a dumped Attribute and the reason the array's zeroes are a decision rather than a formality.

Because the array tops out at 2 for everyone but an **A**, most Level 1 characters spend Skill points on **breadth** rather than depth \- a wide pool has to be spread across many Skills to be spent at all. Depth arrives with the Attribute increases at Levels 4, 8 and 12.

### **Attribute Limits**

**Maximum:** **3 at character creation** \- the top of the **A** array, so it's a ceiling only an **A** reaches. From there the \+1 granted at Levels 4, 8 and 12 (see [[Per Level Advancement|progression_&_rewards]]) can carry one Attribute as high as **5**, the hard ceiling. Racial modifiers sit outside both numbers and can push a score past either.

**Minimum: none.** An Attribute can sit at 0, at \-1, or lower. A 0 is a complete absence of that capacity; a negative score is an active liability, subtracting wherever the Attribute is added (see [[Attributes|core_rules]]). **Nothing in Ressam damages an Attribute**, so a below-0 score only ever comes from a racial modifier landing on an Attribute you assigned a 0 \- it's a character you built, not a character something happened to. Where a negative score would break a derived stat outright, that stat floors instead of going negative: see Step 8.

## Step 6: Choose Knowledge

See the **Knowledge** chapter. Knowledge is separate from your Skill Point budget (Step 5) - it isn't gated by a priority letter, and doesn't compete with Attributes, Skills, or anything else on the ladder from Step 2. You have:

- A **Freebie Pool equal to your MIND score** (as set in Step 5 - snapshotted here, it won't grow if MIND rises later from leveling), spendable on any Knowledge subject.
- Whatever your Race (Step 3) and Career (Step 4) granted - each restricted to that entry's own curated list of subjects.

Familiarity costs 1 point; Expertise costs 3 points total. Points from different sources can combine on the same subject, climbing it toward a higher tier.

## Step 7: Choose Starting Feats

You receive Feats equal to your Feats priority from Step 2 (0 to 4).

- You must meet all prerequisites
- Some feats can be taken multiple times (noted in description)
- Racial and Career skill modifiers count toward prerequisites

See the **Feats** chapter for the complete list.

## Step 8: Calculate Derived Stats

See [[Your Tempo Pool|exchange]] and [[Magic Overview|magic_overview]] for the Tempo Pool and Will formulas in full.

| Stat | Formula |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Wounds** | Size baseline \+ Feats (see [[Carrying Capacity|rest_and_survival]]) |
| **Will** | MIND × 3, minimum 0 |
| **Slots** | 6 \+ STR, minimum 1 |
| **Tempo Pool** | 4, plus your equipped weapon's (and shield's) Attacks modifier - every die a flat `1d12`, refilling in full at the start of the round |
| **Parry** | `1d12` per invested die \+ Weapon Skill \+ Guard |
| **Initiative** | 5 \+ DEX (static, never rolled) |
| **Dent Line / Rend Line** | Equipped armor's printed values |

**Your Tempo Pool is the only resource in a fight** - every attack, every Parry, every Opportunity Attack comes out of it, and there is no free defense of any kind. **No Attribute sizes it any more**: STR is melee damage and nothing else in a fight, and pool size instead comes straight off your gear (see [[Your Tempo Pool|exchange]]).

**Two of these floor rather than go negative** (see Attribute Limits in Step 5): **Will** never drops below **0**, and **Slots** are never fewer than **1** however low STR goes. Everything else takes a negative score straight - a \-1 Ward is a \-1 Ward, a \-1 to melee damage is a real subtraction (a blow reduced to 0 or less inflicts no Wound, see [[Damage Roll|combat]]), and a DEX below 0 gives an Initiative under 5.

---

## Step 9: Choose Equipment

Your career provides starting equipment and currency. Purchase additional items from the **Equipment** chapter.

**Armor Reminder:** Armor Penalty affects Acrobatics and Subterfuge, and **nothing at all inside a fight or in spellcasting** (see [[Armor|armor]]). Nothing reduces it - no Skill and no Feat.

---

## Step 10: Final Details

- **Name:** Appropriate to race and setting
- **Appearance:** Age, build, distinguishing features
- **Personality:** At least one core trait, one flaw, one bond
- **Goals:** Short-term and long-term
- **Connections:** How you know the party, why you're adventuring
