Combat in Ressam is lethal. Armor breaks, injuries accumulate, and death is always one bad decision away.

Ressam is not a game where heroes fight to the last Wound. You won't fight worse for being hurt \- the Wound Penalty only bites outside combat \- but a small pool empties fast, and a big hit can take two Wounds at once. When you've lost half your Wounds, consider tactical withdrawal.

**This chapter is the frame around a fight; [[The Exchange|exchange]] is the fight itself.** Action economy, the damage roll and armor live here. Everything about how an attack is thrown, answered, and paid for \- the Tempo Pool, Distance, Openings, Shock, Initiative, Awareness, Fear and Morale \- lives in [[The Exchange|exchange]].

## Action Economy

| Action Type | Quantity | Examples |
| :---- | :---- |:---------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Major Action** | 1/round | Make an attack, cast a working, Dash, Disengage |
| **Minor Action** | 1/round | Sheathe weapon, drink a draught, open door, reload a missile weapon |
| **Object Interaction** | 1/round | Draw ammunition, take up a thrown weapon, pick up a dropped blade, flip a lever |
| **Free Action** | Unlimited | Drop item, speak briefly, stop sustaining a working, etc |
| **Move Action** | Unlimited | Move up to your maximum Speed, can be broken up into multiples |

- **Per Round:** Refreshes at the start of your *next* turn.
- **Drawing multiple weapons:** As a single Object Interaction, you may draw any number of weapons at once, provided you have a free hand for each one.

**There are no Reactions.** Every off-turn act \- a Parry, an Opportunity Attack, whatever a Feat calls for \- is bought with a **Tempo Die** out of the same pool you attack with (see [[Your Tempo Pool|exchange]]). Your pool is the whole of your off-turn action economy.

## Making an Attack

**An attack is one step of an Exchange, and the Exchange is where it resolves** \- see [[The Exchange|exchange]] for the loop, [[Attacking|exchange]] for the roll, and [[Defending|exchange]] for the Parry that answers it.

### **Damage Roll**

**Roll the weapon's damage dice, summed, `\+ STR`.** This is every melee weapon and every melee Skill without exception, finesse blades included. **A missile weapon adds no Attribute at all** \- a loosed arrow or a shot carries only its own dice.

**Unarmored target:** compare the total to Dent 0 / Rend 5 \- no armor to read, but a real roll all the same (see [[Dent Line and Rend Line|armor]]). Every landing hit is at least 1 Wound; a total of 5 or more is 2.

**Armored target:** compare the total to the target's current Dent Line and Rend Line: below the Dent Line, the blow is **Turned Aside** (no Wound, but it still landed \- see Armor, below); at or above the Dent Line, **1 Wound**; at or above the Rend Line, **2 Wounds**. **If every die in the roll shows its maximum face, the blow found a gap** \- see [[Dent Line and Rend Line|armor]] for what that does.

---

## There Are No Critical Hits

**No roll in Ressam deals double damage, and no margin pays a bonus.** A blow deals what the Dent and Rend Lines say it deals whether the Exchange was won by 5 or by 15, and this is true of a shot and a working alike. **Winning the Exchange by more only ever buys an [[Opening|exchange]]**, never a bigger hit.

**Finding a gap is the one exception, and it doesn't ride the margin.** Every die in a damage roll landing on its maximum face is a fixed die-face outcome \- see [[Dent Line and Rend Line|armor]] \- not a reward for winning by more; a 1-die-invested attack finds a gap exactly as often as an all-in one.

**What a margin buys instead is an [[Opening|exchange]]** \- a Riposte, a Disarm, a Shove \- and the sharper weapons reach one on a smaller margin than the rest (see [[Openings|weapons]]).

## Armor

Armor is just that, your worn armor: it prints two numbers, a Dent Line and a Rend Line, that a landing hit's damage roll is read against \- and it degrades each time you are hit, successfully or not.

### **Dent Line and Rend Line**

When hit:

1. Roll the attack's weapon dice `\+ STR` (missiles: no Attribute) \- see Damage Roll, above
2. Compare the total to your current Dent Line and Rend Line, or Dent 0 / Rend 5 if unarmored
3. Armor loses durability \- see Degradation, below

**Note:** A blow can land, be answered by nobody, and still Turn Aside against fresh heavy armor \- that's coherent with the armor fiction, not a bug. It still costs the target a Tempo Die to Shock, and it still wears the armor down.

### **Degradation**

**Any landed hit reduces worn armor's Dent and Rend Lines by 1, whether or not it deals a Wound.**

| Case | Lines lost |
| :---- | :---- |
| A Blunt hit | 2 \- you ruin the harness rather than open it |
| An Unarmed hit | 1 \- a fist is not a mace |

**Nothing bottoms out below 0.**

| Status | Effect |
| :---- | :---- |
| Dent 0 (**Broken**) | Resolves as Dent 0 / Rend 5 \- exactly like no armor |
| Rend 0 too (**Destroyed**) | Every hit is 2 Wounds \- worse than no armor |

Broken or Destroyed armor keeps its Speed penalty. Taking armor off costs a Major Action. See [[Rest \& Repair|rest_and_survival]] for how it comes back.

### **Armor Penalty**

**Nothing in a fight reads Armor Penalty.** It applies to Acrobatics and Subterfuge checks, and it is derived from your armor's Dent Line \- see [[Armor|armor]].
