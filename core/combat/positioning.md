Where creatures stand relative to each other shapes an exchange as much as the roll does. This chapter owns the grid, movement, and Cover; see [[The Exchange|exchange]] for Distance and Weapon Length \- how far the thing in your hand reaches from where you're standing, and what it can do about a blow coming the other way \- and [[Combat|combat]] for action economy and the damage roll.

---

## Battlemap & Positioning

Ressam is played on a grid of 5-foot squares, minis-and-battlemap style \- every Speed, Reach, and Range value in this book is already denominated in that unit.

- **Size & Squares:** A creature occupies a number of squares matching its Space ([[Size|carrying_and_resting]]) \- Small/Medium: 1 square, Large: 2x2, Huge: 3x3.
- **Measuring Distance:** Count outward square-by-square from your square to the nearest square of the target. Every square costs the same to enter, whether moved orthogonally or diagonally.
- **Areas of Effect:** Count outward from the origin square the same way; a radius reaches every square its count extends to, no partial-coverage rulings needed.

**Melee distance is set by what's in your hands, not a fixed pair of ranges.** Every weapon carries a **Length** from 1 to 5, printed on it, and Length decides how far it reaches - Length 1-3 fight **Adjacent** (0 squares between you), Length 4 reaches 1 square, and the pike alone (Length 5) reaches 1-2 squares. See [[Weapon Length|exchange]].

## Movement in Combat

- **Move:** Move up to your speed; can split before/after action
- **Dash:** Major Action for additional movement equal to speed
- **Disengage:** Major Action to avoid Opportunity Attacks this turn \- see [[Leaving a fight|exchange]] for what that buys and what it costs
- **Difficult Terrain:** Costs double movement
- **Standing from Prone:** Costs your whole Move Action

**Opportunity Attacks** are bought with a Tempo Die and thrown at the Distance your weapon threatens \- which squares those are, and what happens when a body stands in one of them, is in [[What you threaten|exchange]].

## Cover

Cover in Ressam is a binary system \- you either have it or you don't. Your DM has final say on if your position is in cover or not.

**Cover:** Completely hidden behind solid obstruction. Cannot be targeted by attacks or most spells.

**No Partial Cover:** Any exposure \= targetable. A low wall, a tree trunk, and an ally standing in the line all grant nothing at all against a shot \- see the [[Shot DC|exchange]] table, which Cover never appears on.

**Cover doesn't protect against:** AoE originating behind cover, spells without line of sight, attackers who reposition.

**A body in the way is not Cover, and only the pike treats it specially.** Every Length 1-3 weapon fights Adjacent and every Length 4 weapon reaches exactly the one square in front of it - neither has a square in between to be blocked from. The pike (Length 5) is the only weapon with a two-square reach, and a creature standing in its one intervening square does not block it: the pike acts through that square as though it were empty. That is a fact about haft length rather than concealment; see [[Fighting outside your Length|exchange]].

**Cover blocks ranged attacks and Spell Attacks, not melee.** A creature wholly behind solid obstruction - a deployed Pavise, a wall - cannot be shot or targeted by a Spell Attack (see [[There is no defense against a ranged attack|exchange]]). It says nothing about a melee attacker who has closed to Adjacent or Reach around the obstruction: whether a blade can actually reach a target hiding behind cover is a question of Distance and Weapon Length, not of Cover.
