Where creatures stand relative to each other shapes an exchange as much as the roll does. This chapter owns the grid, movement, and Cover; see [[The Exchange|exchange]] for Distance and Reach \- how far the thing in your hand reaches from where you're standing, and what it can do about a blow coming the other way \- and [[Combat|combat]] for action economy and the damage roll.

---

## Battlemap & Positioning

Ressam is played on a grid of 5-foot squares, minis-and-battlemap style \- every Speed and Range value in this book is already denominated in that unit.

- **Size & Squares:** A creature occupies a number of squares matching its Space ([[Size|rest_and_survival]]) \- Small/Medium: 1 square, Large: 2x2, Huge: 3x3.
- **Measuring Distance:** Count outward square-by-square from your square to the nearest square of the target. Every square costs the same to enter, whether moved orthogonally or diagonally.
- **Areas of Effect:** Count outward from the origin square the same way; a radius reaches every square its count extends to, no partial-coverage rulings needed.

**Melee distance is set by what's in your hands, not a fixed pair of ranges.** Every weapon carries a **Reach** \- Normal (Adjacent only), Reach 1 (Adjacent through 1 square), or Reach 2 (Adjacent through 2 squares). See [[Distance \& Reach|exchange]].

## Movement in Combat

- **Move:** Move up to your Speed; can split before/after an action.
- **Dash:** Major Action for additional movement equal to Speed.
- **Disengage:** Major Action to avoid Opportunity Attacks this turn \- see [[Distance \& Reach|exchange]] for what that buys and what it costs.
- **Difficult Terrain:** Costs double movement.
- **Standing from Prone:** Costs your whole Move Action.

**Opportunity Attacks** are bought with a Tempo Die and thrown at the Reach your weapon threatens \- which squares those are, and what happens when a body stands in one of them, is in [[Opportunity Attacks|exchange]].

## Cover

Cover in Ressam is a binary system \- you either have it or you don't. Your GM has final say on whether a position is in cover.

**Cover:** Completely hidden behind solid obstruction. Cannot be targeted by shots or most workings.

**No Partial Cover:** Any exposure \= targetable. A low wall, a tree trunk, and an ally standing in the line all read as **Light cover** or **Heavy cover** on the [[Shot DC|exchange]] table instead \- see that table for the exact bands.

**Cover doesn't protect against:** area effects originating behind cover, workings without line of sight, attackers who reposition.

**A body in the way is not Cover, and only Reach 2 treats it specially.** Normal Reach fights Adjacent and Reach 1 reaches exactly the one square in front of it \- neither has a square in between to be blocked from. Reach 2 is the only threat with a two-square span, and a creature standing in its one intervening square does not block it: the attack acts through that square as though it were empty. See [[Distance \& Reach|exchange]].

**Cover blocks shots and workings, not melee.** A creature wholly behind solid obstruction \- a deployed Pavise, a wall \- cannot be shot or targeted by a working (see [The shot](exchange#the-shot)). It says nothing about a melee attacker who has closed to Adjacent or Reach around the obstruction: whether a blade can actually reach a target hiding behind cover is a question of Distance and Reach, not of Cover.
