Magic schools are Feats, not Skills. See [[Magic Overview|magic_overview]]'s Casting section for the roll itself; this chapter is only how a caster buys into a school in the first place.

**A Magic Feat is an access tax, not a toolkit.** Compare [[Martial Feats|martial_feats]], where a Feat slot expands what you can actually *do* in a fight - a new maneuver, a wider Opening, a sharper edge case. A Magic Feat does none of that: its entire job is permission to learn and cast a school's workings at a given difficulty. A caster's actual power grows through MIND, Will, and workings known (see [[Learning Workings|magic_overview]]), never through the Feat itself.

**One template, ten schools.** Every school - Aeromancy, Geomancy, Hydromancy, Pyromancy, Shadowmancy, Benediction, Cultivation, Invocation, Necration, Subjugation - takes the same four Feats below, substituting its own name for `[School]`. Nothing else about them changes from school to school.

---

### **Novice \[School\]**

You can hold a working's shape in your mind long enough to loose it without it slipping from your grasp.

**Prerequisites:** Level 1
**Benefit:** You may learn and cast `[School]` workings of Lesser difficulty (5+).

### **Adept \[School\]**

The working stops fighting you on the way out - you shape it now, rather than merely surviving it.

**Prerequisites:** Level 3, Novice `[School]`
**Benefit:** You may learn and cast `[School]` workings of Common difficulty (7+) and below.

### **Expert \[School\]**

You no longer wrestle the working into being. You tell it where it already wanted to go.

**Prerequisites:** Level 5, Adept `[School]`
**Benefit:** You may learn and cast `[School]` workings of Greater difficulty (9+) and below.

### **Master \[School\]**

The working answers before you finish the thought.

**Prerequisites:** Level 8, Expert `[School]`
**Benefit:** You may learn and cast `[School]` workings of any difficulty, Legendary (11+) included.

---

**Master is the ceiling.** Nothing sits above it - a character's magic stops deepening at Level 8, and Feats from there on go to General, Martial, Skill, Prestige, or a different school's own ladder.

**The four tiers form a straight line within a school** - you cannot skip Novice to take Adept, or Adept to take Expert, or Expert to take Master - but there's no cap on how many *different* schools' ladders you climb at once beyond the Feat slots it costs; the Feat economy is the only limiter (see `feats_overview.md`'s note on prerequisites vs. slot scarcity).
