These feats are available to all characters and represent a wide variety of broadly applicable abilities.

---

## Awareness & Reflexes

### **Alert**

You are always on guard and difficult to surprise.

**Prerequisites:** \+2 ranks in Perception  
**Benefit:** You have advantage on Perception checks to avoid being surprised, and hidden creatures don't gain advantage on attacks against you. You also have \+3 to DEX Wards against effects you can see, such as traps and spells. Once you have \+3 ranks in Perception, you cannot be surprised at all while conscious and not incapacitated.

---

## Willpower & Fortune

### **Lucky**

Fortune favors you in your endeavors.

**Prerequisites:** CHA 4+  
**Benefit:** You have 3 luck points. When you make an attack roll, Skill check, or Ward check, you may spend 1 luck point to reroll a die and choose which result to use. You regain all luck points after a Field Rest or Long Rest.

---

### **Indomitable**

Your will allows you to shrug off effects that would fell others.

**Prerequisites:** END 4+, \+2 ranks in Athletics  
**Benefit:** When you fail a Ward check, you may choose to reroll it and must use the new result. Additionally, while you are below half your maximum Wounds, you gain \+3 to all STR and END Wards.

---

## Utility & Social

### **Inspiring Leader**

Your encouragement helps allies push through pain and fatigue.

**Prerequisites:** CHA 3+  
**Benefit:** You can spend 10 minutes encouraging your allies. Choose up to 6 friendly creatures (including yourself) who can see or hear you. For the next hour, each creature ignores half their penalty from their Trauma (a creature at Trauma 4 functions as Trauma 2\) (rounded down). This does not reduce actual Trauma levels. A creature can't benefit from this again until they finish a Field Rest or Long Rest.

---

## Magical Utility

### **Unbreakable Focus**

You are a being of unshakable will and unbending resolve.

**Prerequisites:** END 3+, MIND 3+ or FAI 3+, \+2 ranks in any skill under that attribute  
**Benefit:** When you are targeted by an overcome roll you are aware of, you may use your Reaction to gain \+4 to your Ward against it. If the overcome still succeeds, you may choose to suffer 1 Trauma to reduce it to the Resist effect instead. Once per round.
