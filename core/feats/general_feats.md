These feats are available to all characters and represent broadly applicable training - real habits of awareness, grit, and command that don't require specialized weapon or magical training to pick up.

**Written in the same keyword-ability shape as Martial Feats:** a flavor line describing a real technique plainly (no named school, master, or nation), then a Benefit stated as a single trigger/cost/effect clause. Where an existing term already covers the mechanic (Advantage, a free die, a Ward, a Resolve check, Steady the Line), the Benefit invokes it by name rather than re-describing it.

---

### **Watchful**

*A sentry's habit - always tracking exits, hands, and distance.*

**Prerequisites:** +1 rank in Perception

**Benefit:** Advantage on Perception checks to notice a threat.

---

### **Second Wind**

*The adrenaline that keeps a wounded body moving past where it should have stopped.*

**Prerequisites:** STR 1+

**Benefit:** While below half your maximum Wounds, Advantage on STR and DEX Wards.

---

### **Tough**

*A body that's simply taken more punishment before, and adapted to keep taking it.*

**Prerequisites:** None

**Benefit:** +1 Wounds.

---

### **Command Presence**

*A voice pitched to carry over the noise of a fight, and worth listening to when it does.*

**Prerequisites:** +3 ranks in Leadership

**Benefit:** Steady the Line has no range limit for you.

---

### **Hardened**

*A calm that isn't bravery - just having already lived through worse.*

**Prerequisites:** Hold at least 1 Scar - or, taken at character creation only, start play with 1 Wound already marked off instead.

**Benefit:** Advantage on Resolve checks.

---

### **Iron Nerve**

*A steadiness drilled into soldiers who have to function through bombardment and worse.*

**Prerequisites:** MIND 2+

**Benefit:** 1 free die on Fear checks.

---

### **Combat Medic**

*Field triage - trained to work fast when a wasted second is a lost one.*

**Prerequisites:** +3 ranks in Chirurgery

**Benefit:** Reviving a Downed ally costs a Lesser Action instead of a Major Action, and heals 1 additional Wound on a success.

---

### **Deep Reserves**

*An endurance built through years of hard labor or long marches, with one more push held in reserve.*

**Prerequisites:** +1 rank in Athletics

**Benefit:** Your first Exert each fight costs no Trauma.
