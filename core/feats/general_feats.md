These feats are available to all characters and represent broadly applicable training - real habits of awareness, grit, and command that don't require specialized weapon or magical training to pick up.

---

## Awareness & Reflexes

### **Combat Awareness**

You've drilled yourself to always be scanning - exits, hands, distances - the same habit that keeps sentries and watchmen from ever being caught flat-footed.

**Prerequisites:** \+2 ranks in Perception  
**Benefit:** You have advantage on Perception checks to avoid being surprised, and hidden creatures don't gain advantage on attacks against you. You also have \+3 to DEX Wards against effects you can see, such as traps and spells. Once you have \+3 ranks in Perception, you cannot be surprised at all while conscious and not incapacitated.

## Willpower & Grit

### **Second Wind**

Adrenaline doesn't care that you're hurt. Fighters half-dead from blood loss have carried allies out of worse.

**Prerequisites:** END 4+, \+2 ranks in Athletics  
**Benefit:** When you fail a Ward check, you may choose to reroll it and must use the new result. Additionally, while you are below half your maximum Wounds, you gain \+3 to all STR and END Wards.

---

### **Veteran's Instinct**

You've been in enough fights that your gut works out the answer before your head catches up - and you've learned to trust it over your first read.

**Prerequisites:** \+2 ranks in Perception or Insight  
**Benefit:** You have 3 Instinct points. When you make an attack roll, Skill check, or Ward check, you may spend 1 Instinct point to reroll a die and choose which result to use. You regain all Instinct points after a Field Rest or Long Rest.

---

## Utility & Social

### **Inspiring Leader**

Your encouragement helps allies push through pain and fatigue - the same pep talk any officer gives troops before a hard push.

**Prerequisites:** CHA 3+  
**Benefit:** You can spend 10 minutes encouraging your allies. Choose up to 6 friendly creatures (including yourself) who can see or hear you. For the next hour, each creature ignores half their penalty from their Trauma (a creature at Trauma 4 functions as Trauma 2\) (rounded down). This does not reduce actual Trauma levels. A creature can't benefit from this again until they finish a Field Rest or Long Rest.

---

## Composure Under Fire

### **Stress Inoculation**

Repeated exposure to controlled danger - drills run hot, sparring past exhaustion - trains the panic out of your first response.

**Prerequisites:** END 3+, \+2 ranks in Athletics or Survival  
**Benefit:** When you fail a Ward check against an effect meant to frighten, panic, or overwhelm you (including a spell's Overcome roll targeting you), you may use your Reaction to gain \+4 to that Ward and reroll it. If it still fails, you may choose to suffer 1 Trauma to shake off the effect immediately instead of enduring its full duration. Once per round.
