These feats focus on combat prowess, weapon mastery, and tactical battlefield abilities.

---

## Weapon Mastery

### **Deadly Critical**

Your critical strikes can cripple even the hardiest opponents.

**Prerequisites:** \+3 ranks in weapon Skill  
**Benefit:** Your critical hit range with a chosen weapon skill increases by 1 (12 becomes 11-12 on 1d12).  
**Special:** This feat applies to only one weapon skill category. You may select this feat multiple times; each time it applies to a different weapon skill.

---

## Ranged Combat

### **Gunner**

You keep your hands steady, your powder dry, and your reloading drill free of wasted motion.

**Prerequisites:** \+2 ranks in any firearm Skill  
**Benefit:** As a Minor Action, provided you have not moved this turn, gain Advantage on your next firearm attack this turn. Your Speed is reduced to 0 until the end of the current turn..

---

## Defensive Combat

### **Deflect Missiles**

You can knock aside incoming projectiles with lightning reflexes.

**Prerequisites:** PRE 3+, \+2 ranks in Brawling or Acrobatics  
**Benefit:** When you are hit by a ranged weapon attack, you may use your Reaction to reduce the damage by 1d10 \+ Brawling ranks. If you reduce the damage to 0, the projectile is harmlessly deflected.  
**Restriction:** You must have at least one free hand. Cannot be used while wearing Rigid Armor.

---

## Weapon Handling

### **Quickdraw**

Your hands move with blinding speed.

**Prerequisites:** DEX 3+, PRE 3+  
**Benefit:** You can draw or sheathe any number of weapons as part of making an attack (free action). When combat begins, you act first in any initiative tie.
