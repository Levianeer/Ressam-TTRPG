These feats focus on combat prowess, weapon mastery, and tactical battlefield abilities - real techniques of the blade, the gun, and the guard, not fantastical tricks.

**Each Feat below is written as a keyword ability, not a paragraph.** Every Feat is still traceable to a real historical technique, but the flavor line describes the technique plainly - one sentence, no named school, master, or nation - fitting a fantasy setting rather than reading as a HEMA citation. The Benefit itself is a single trigger/cost/effect clause - no restated context, no scenario-setting. Where an existing term already covers the mechanic (an Opening, Armor-Piercing, Reach, Shock, an Opportunity Attack), the Benefit invokes it by name rather than re-describing it; a new keyword only gets minted when nothing on the books already fits.

---

## Keywords

**In a Bind:** two fighters are In a Bind whenever an Exchange between them ends in a tie.

---

### **Crossing Guard**

*A crossing cut thrown to answer a bind instead of yielding it.*

**Prerequisites:** +1 rank in a STR melee weapon Skill

**Benefit:** When In a Bind, spend 1 Tempo Die: make a Riposte.

---

### **Half-Sword Grip**

*Gripping the blade itself for the gaps in plate, not the power of a swing.*

**Prerequisites:** +2 ranks in Two-Handed Blades

**Benefit:** Lesser Action: grip at half-sword (Armor-Piercing 2, no Signature Opening) until you re-grip.

---

### **Underhook**

*A wrestler's grip that's already halfway to the throw.*

**Prerequisites:** +1 rank in Daggers & Wrestling

**Benefit:** Your Grapple Opening also knocks Prone.

---

### **Set the Line**

*The pike hedge - the tactic that ended cavalry's monopoly on the charge.*

**Prerequisites:** +3 ranks in Polearms

**Benefit:** Your attacks with a Reach 1 or Reach 2 Polearm gain Armor-Piercing 3.

---

### **Concussive Blow**

*The war hammer and maul - built to rattle the man inside the harness, not just dent it.*

**Prerequisites:** +2 ranks in Hafted Weapons

**Benefit:** Your Blunt hits with a Hafted weapon that are Turned Aside cost the target 1 additional Tempo Die.

---

### **Double-Shotted**

*The gunner's trick of doubling a charge to punch through plate a single ball won't.*

**Prerequisites:** +2 ranks in Firearms

**Benefit:** Lesser Action to load: your next shot gains Armor-Piercing +2 and doubles this weapon's Misfire score.

---

### **Blade's Answer**

*A riposte thrown in the same tempo as the parry, no pause between them.*

**Prerequisites:** +3 ranks in Fencing Blades

**Benefit:** Your Ripostes with a Fencing Blades weapon: first three dice free instead of one.
