These feats represent mastery of specific non-combat abilities and specialized training - real trades and disciplines, sharpened past what raw Skill ranks alone represent.

**Written in the same keyword-ability shape as Martial and General Feats:** a flavor line describing a real trade plainly (no named school, master, or nation), then a Benefit stated as a single trigger/cost/effect clause. Where an existing term already covers the mechanic (Advantage, Batch Size, a Long Rest repair check, Cohort Level), the Benefit invokes it by name rather than re-describing it.

---

### **Tradecraft**

*A specialist's narrow mastery of one trade's tools - tension wrench and pick, not the whole trade at once.*

**Prerequisites:** +1 rank in Subterfuge

**Benefit:** Choose lockpicking, sleight of hand, or moving unseen. Advantage on Subterfuge checks of that kind. Can be taken more than once, choosing a different kind each time.

---

### **Tracker**

*A trained eye for disturbed ground, broken brush, and a print's age.*

**Prerequisites:** +2 ranks in Survival

**Benefit:** Advantage on Survival checks to follow or age a trail.

---

### **Apothecary**

*A chemist's efficient hand with reagents - nothing measured out goes to waste.*

**Prerequisites:** +3 ranks in Thaumaturgy

**Benefit:** Your Batch Size is 1 + Thaumaturgy Skill, not Thaumaturgy ÷ 2.

---

### **Armorer's Eye**

*A smith's practiced sense for exactly how much heat and hammering a repair needs.*

**Prerequisites:** +2 ranks in Crafting

**Benefit:** A successful Long Rest repair check restores 3 points to each line instead of 2.

---

### **Read the Room**

*A negotiator's habit - watch the hands and the eyes, not just the words.*

**Prerequisites:** +1 rank in Insight

**Benefit:** Advantage on Insight checks to detect a lie or hidden motive.

---

### **Second-in-Command**

*A trainer's knack for building someone up faster than experience alone would.*

**Prerequisites:** +2 ranks in Leadership

**Benefit:** Your Cohort's Level is your Level minus 1 instead of minus 2.

---

### **Horse Sense**

*A rider's feel for a mount's temper, steady enough to settle it before it bolts.*

**Prerequisites:** +1 rank in Wayfaring

**Benefit:** Advantage on Wayfaring checks to control a panicked mount.
