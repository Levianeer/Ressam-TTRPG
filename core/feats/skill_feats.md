These feats represent mastery of specific non-combat abilities and specialized training.

---

## Stealth & Subterfuge

### **Shadow Stalker**

You move like a ghost through darkness and shadow.

**Prerequisites:** \+3 ranks in Stealth  
**Benefit:** You can attempt to hide even when lightly obscured. You have advantage on Stealth checks while in dim light or darkness. Moving up to half your speed doesn't impose disadvantage on Stealth checks.

---

## Social Skills

### **Intimidating Presence**

Your threats can break the will of even hardened warriors.

**Prerequisites:** \+3 ranks in Intimidation, CHA 3+ or STR 3+  
**Benefit:** As a Major Action, make an Intimidation check against the CHA Ward of one target within 30 ft who can see or hear you (Contested Ward). If it beats their Ward, they become frightened of you for 1 minute. Frightened creatures can repeat the Ward at the end of each of their turns (with advantage if they are more than 30 ft away from you). You may use this ability a number of times per Field Rest or Long Rest equal to your CHA or STR (whichever you use for Intimidation).

---

### **Insightful**

You can read people like books.

**Prerequisites:** \+3 ranks in Insight  
**Benefit:** Creatures who roll Deception against you have disadvantage. You have advantage on Insight checks. You can read surface emotions of creatures you interact with.

---

## Crafting Skills

### **Expert Alchemist**

You can create potions and alchemical substances with ease.

**Prerequisites:** \+2 ranks in Alchemy  
**Benefit:** You can craft potions, poisons, and alchemical items at half the normal cost and time. Once per Field Rest or Long Rest, you may create one potion of a formula you know without spending materials.
