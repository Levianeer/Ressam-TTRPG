Trere is Ressam's most south-western continent, shifting through a full gradient of biomes from the northern dunes of the Great Sand down to southern temperate rainforest and wetland. Home to Mafsoleios and the Gilded Hwispian Maw's Svarnanagaram, with Alashar Tribes and Desert Nomads ranging between them. See [[Geography|geography]] for the continent's full overview.
