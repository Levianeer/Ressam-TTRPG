Lustralis is Ressam's largest western continent - sprawling desert, coastal rainforest, and savannah around the Great Salt Expanse. Human kingdoms and Alsahli nomads share the land; Varulf haunt the misted inland woods. See [[Geography|geography]] for the continent's full overview.
