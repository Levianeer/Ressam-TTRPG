Gelidia is a large island continent between Aurkhan and Terrevault - frozen tundra, glaciers, and icy fjords under near-perpetual blizzard. Dzinari sanctuaries and Feliid snow-forests are its only settled reaches. See [[Geography|geography]] for the continent's full overview.
