Inggaz is a southern continent joined to Terrevault - molten plains and lava fields in the north, frigid extremes in the south. Orkh warbands and Khoridae citadels hold the land, and the Infernal Gate is said to open onto the Fields of Nocturne. See [[Geography|geography]] for the continent's full overview.
