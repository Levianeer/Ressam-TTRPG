## Magic rework Stage 1 is done - Stage 2 (propagation) is open

**2026-09-14, Stage 1 (chassis):** the Arcane/Divine split is gone. ARC and FAI are deleted as Attributes; the standard array shrank from six numbers to four (new arrays: A 3,2,1,0 / B 2,2,1,0 / C 2,1,1,0 / D 1,1,1,0 / E 1,1,0,0). Magic school Skills are deleted outright - no Skill or Attribute is ever added to a casting roll. Schools are now Feats: a Novice/Adept/Expert **Unlock** per school (spell access, gated by Level and a linear same-school prerequisite chain), a matching **Focus** (flat +1/+2/+3 roll bonus, non-stacking, gated behind its Unlock), and **Combination** Feats (a spell spanning multiple schools, gated behind the matching tier's Unlock in every contributing school) - see the new `core/feats/magic_feats.md`, which replaces the now-deleted `arcane_feats.md`/`divine_feats.md`. Casting itself is now one roll for every school: `1d12` vs. `5 + spell rank` (rank = the old 1-5 Mana Cost scale), Feat bonus only, flat 1 Mana per cast regardless of rank, nothing on failure, no Armor Penalty. A targeted/thrown-shape spell can be dodged (defender spends a Tempo Die, opposed roll, no modifier); a mind/body/will-shape spell cannot be dodged at all. Faith survives as pure in-fiction flavor for devotion-shaped schools (Benediction, Cultivation, Invocation, Necration, Subjugation) with zero mechanical weight - no devotion-gated Mana recovery, no Rite Mastery reroll charges (deleted outright), no scribing (deleted outright - every school now casts straight from Mana). Updated for consistency: `core_rules.md`, `attributes_and_skills.md`, `character_creation.md`, `magic_overview.md` (full rewrite), `laws_of_magic.md` (Reciprocity reworded to flavor), `minor_magic.md`, `armor.md` (Penalty no longer touches spellcasting anywhere it was mentioned), `DESIGN_GUIDE.md` (Overcomes/Faith Spell Guidelines rewritten, Arcane & Divine Feat Guidelines merged into one Magic Feat Guidelines section), `feats_overview.md`, `templates/Home.md` and `templates/_Sidebar.md`.

**Stage 2, races: done (2026-09-14).** Policy: every ARC/FAI point redirects to MIND (not dropped) - MIND absorbed FAI's old mechanical role wherever it appeared, including two spots that weren't flat modifiers (Daemons' Possession/Whispers of Unmaking rolled against a `FAI Ward`, now `MIND Ward`; Aelves' Flesh Communion rolled a `FAI check`, now a `MIND check`). Changed: Dzinari (+1 ARC → +1 MIND), Khoridae (-1 ARC/-1 FAI → -2 MIND), Varulf (-1 FAI → -1 MIND), Tembels (+1 FAI → folded into its existing +1 MIND for +2 MIND), Tapio (+1 FAI canceled against its existing -1 MIND, netting to just +1 STR/-1 CHA), Daemons (its 2-point mental-Attribute pool narrowed from MIND/ARC/FAI/CHA to MIND/CHA, same 2 points). Tapio's `+2 in two FAI Skills` Starting Skills line was dropped outright with nothing granted in its place (explicit call, not a default). Aelves' and Tapio's racial free-cast features ("cast X once per Rest without needing ranks in the skill") reworded to "without needing that school's Unlock Feat." **Not re-litigated:** whether any of these nets a race weaker or stronger than before - flag for playtesting, not solved here.

## The Exchange is merged but unplaytested - open tuning questions

- **STR/DEX sits ~12.7 points to DEX under optimal play** (extra dice buy offense and defense; die size only buys quality). Shallow slope, no build below 42%/above 56%, accepted rather than fixed - revisit if real play disagrees.
- **An archer's win rate swings ~45 points on the GM's choice of opening distance** (42% at standoff 0, 82% at standoff 3). Worth a table-facing guideline once there's play data.
- **Armor has no in-fight counterweight** now that nothing reads Penalty - intended, but never explicitly decided as the reason. Still true after the 2026-08-30 Dent/Rend Line rework (`exchange_log.md` item 66): heavier armor's lines are still strictly higher than lighter armor's, same ordering AR had. If plate needs holding back later, it has to be a Dent/Rend Line or durability change.
- **Never measured:** the Shot DC table, the Mythic pool size, the Called Shot menu's rate, and the four new Tempo/Openings Feats (Cleave Through, Economy of Motion, Set Yourself, Practiced Loader).
- **Changed at merge, not re-simulated:** the Blinded ruling, the Size-to-Band remap, and the three rebuilt Feats that now move a weapon a Band (Adaptive Guard, Half-Swording, Shortened Grip).
- **Changed 2026-08-30, not simulated at all** (`exchange_log.md` items 64-65): melee dropped multiple declared attacks per turn for one attack with dice invested and summed into it (Parry got the same treatment); ranged weapons lost the Tempo Pool entirely - a shot is now a flat `1d12 + Skill` vs. Shot DC, firing and reloading are pure action economy. Both were player-directed rulings, not measurements - no win-rate, fight-length, or archer-viability numbers exist for either yet, and `tools/tempo_sim.py` cannot produce them until it's rebuilt (see the tooling item above).

## Arcane and Divine spell lists need a rebalance and expansion pass

The Petition Roll rework changed how every Divine spell resolves, but damage dice/effect magnitudes/Mana Costs still carry pre-Petition-Roll numbers. Arcane has its own longstanding staleness independent of that. Needs real playtest data, not a desk pass - treat both lists as one rebalance, not two, since Distinction between the two paths is the point.

**Also scoped into this pass now that Spell Crafting is gone** (deleted outright 2026-08-29 as a system - see `CLAUDE.md`'s `core/magic/` bullet):

- **Expand every school to ~10 spells.** Most schools sit at 6 today (Shadowmancy is the outlier at 8) - custom spell-building was the pressure valve for a thin list, and without it the printed lists need to carry more of the weight themselves.
- **Make the schools more restrictive and less trivial to spread across.** Nothing meaningfully discourages a caster from picking up spells in several schools at once right now; tighten whatever gates that (Skill investment, a prerequisite, a slot/point cost) so specializing in one or two schools is a real choice rather than free breadth.
