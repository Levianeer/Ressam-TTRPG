## combat_draft.md audit - closed, five confirmed bugs fixed

User-directed full audit of `exchange.md`, `combat.md`, `positioning.md`, and `rest_and_survival.md` against `combat_draft.md` (and `equipment_draft.md` where it bears on the same mechanics), triggered by a caught error in a design conversation (melee dice were assumed to sum, and didn't). Five real divergences were found and fixed:

1. **Melee Tempo Dice were summed instead of taking the highest.** `combat_draft.md` says, twice, explicitly: "Take the highest die you rolled, not the sum." `exchange.md` had it as "summed" in the Exchange table (both Defend and Attack steps) and the Parry row of Defending - the core resolution method for every Parry and Attack in the game, silently wrong. Fixed in all three spots, plus a new explicit top-of-file statement in the Tempo Pool section so it can't drift again unnoticed: melee, a shot, and a cast all take the highest die, none of them sum.
2. **The Conditions table's Prone and Restrained entries were pre-rework leftovers, self-contradictory with the rest of the chapter.** Prone read "Parries at no penalty" and invented "Disadvantage on attack rolls; attacks against it have Advantage" - the opposite of the draft's actual "at most 1 die in each Parry, attacking unaffected," and it directly contradicted `rest_and_survival.md`'s own Downed section a few lines later, which correctly said a Downed (Prone) character can only Parry with 1 die. Restrained had the same invented Advantage/Disadvantage attack-roll language despite the draft saying "attacking... unaffected." Both fixed to match the draft exactly. **Not yet audited:** the rest of the Conditions table (Bleeding, Blinded, Broken, Charmed, Deafened, Flying, Frightened, Incapacitated, Invisible, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Silenced, Stunned, Unconscious) has no `combat_draft.md` equivalent to check against, but several of them (Blinded, Frightened, Invisible, Poisoned) carry the same stale "advantage/disadvantage on attack rolls" language that doesn't map onto the Exchange's dice-investment model. Fixing those needs new design decisions, not a restore-from-draft - flagged here for a dedicated pass, not fixed in this session.
3. **Long Rest's bonus-healing DC was a tier low.** Draft: Chirurgery/Smithing check DC 9 for the extra Wound/armor point. Book had DC 7. Fixed to DC 9.
4. **Every Polearm was missing "Ignores Guard."** Both `combat_draft.md` ("Reach 1 and Reach 2 weapons Ignore Guard") and `equipment_draft.md` (with an explicit worked Rapier-vs-Spear cost comparison: "one buys Reach and Ignores Guard, the other buys the knife through the visor - that's the trade") state this as a deliberate weapon-balance point. `weapons.md` only gave "Ignores Guard" to the Chain Flail (a separate, legitimately-logged reason). Added to Spear, Pike, Halberd, Glaive, Quarterstaff, and Lance's Properties, plus a rule statement in the Reach section and an updated Shields-section note that no longer reads as Chain-Flail-exclusive.
5. **`exchange.md` kept its own copy of the Reach-to-weapon table, and it had drifted wrong** - Reach 1 listed only "Halberd, Glaive" (missing Spear, Quarterstaff, Lance) and Normal Reach was described as "every one-handed or Light melee weapon" (wrongly excluding Two-Handed Normal-Reach weapons like the Greatsword or War Maul). `weapons.md`'s own table was already correct. Fixed by deleting the duplicate weapon list from `exchange.md` and pointing to `weapons.md` instead, so there's only one copy to drift.

One more small gap, not draft-sourced but confirmed by the draft's own explicit list: Downed characters weren't barred from Riposte in `rest_and_survival.md`, though the draft explicitly lists it among what a Downed creature can't do. Fixed.

**Checked against this fix:** the Martial Feats drafted the same session (`martial_feats.md`) were reasoned against the broken "summed" math before this was caught. None of their wording actually hardcoded a sum-based assumption (they reference "Riposte," "free dice," Armor-Piercing, etc. by name rather than restating the math), so all seven hold up unchanged now that the underlying mechanic is fixed - Blade's Answer in particular reads as a clean, correctly-scaled bonus now (roughly an Advantage-equivalent on a Riposte) rather than the "maybe too strong" flag it got under the wrong model.

## Spell-file conversion: planning notes - not started, no spells written yet

User-directed planning session, explicitly scoped to *not* write any converted spells - the goal was to work out what the ten schools should look like under the MESBG-derived Will/tier system before actually doing it. Supersedes the old "expand to ~10 spells" note below with a settled shape. Read all ten current school files plus `spells_draft.md`'s fifteen examples and `DESIGN_GUIDE.md`'s Spell Design Guidelines to produce this.

### Every current spell breaks on one or more of these - this is a redesign pass, not a renaming one

1. **No damage type on any working, ever** (`magic_overview.md`: "Workings carry no damage type"). Every current spell is Fire/Cold/Necrotic/Radiant/Lightning/Psychic with mechanical Resistance/Vulnerability hooks - a school's elemental identity now has to live in fiction and effect design (a Burning rider, say), never a type tag.
2. **No stat added to damage, ever.** Every current spell rolls `+ARC`/`+FAI`. New damage is bare dice only, and small - `1d4`-`2d10` is the observed range across all four tiers in `spells_draft.md`, not a floor to build up from.
3. **Mana Cost 1-5 does not map 1:1 onto Lesser/Common/Greater/Legendary.** Four tiers, not five, and tiers buy scope/duration, not bigger numbers - a "cost 3" spell could land at Common or Greater depending on what it does, not its old price.
4. **Petition Roll (DC 7) is gone.** Every Divine spell currently ignores its own cost and rolls a flat DC 7 - that's the pre-unification mechanic. Divine now casts exactly like Arcane: tiered DC, Will invested, highest die taken. Biggest single change for the five Divine files.
5. **FAI is gone.** No casting stat ever adds to a roll or damage total.
6. **A hostile working is never resisted by a Ward** - always the target's own Will roll, matching or beating the caster's highest die.
7. **Patched Wounds is gone.** Every heal becomes a direct Wound restore, Trauma-taxed per Wound.
8. **Advantage/disadvantage riders need Free Dice or need to die** - magic has its own vocabulary for this (see `magic_overview.md`'s Free Dice), not the general Advantage mechanic, and "advantage on an attack roll" is the exact stale pattern the `combat_draft.md` audit above already found and fixed elsewhere - don't reintroduce it here.
9. **Flat real-time durations ("1 hour," "24 hours") become Sustained or Held Will**, not a kept clock.
10. **"Duration: Channel" is a naming collision** with the new Channelling casting technique - old "channel to sustain" = new **Sustained**.
11. **Multi-modal, many-claused spells need real simplification**, not conversion - Apotheosis, Soul Armament (3 sub-choices), Gaze of the Sharadhi (3 modes), Constructed Perception don't fit the one-clause shape every `spells_draft.md` example uses.
12. **Reactive/off-turn casting - resolved, see below.**

### Three shape decisions made this session

- **Reactive/off-turn workings cost 1 Tempo Die, in addition to committing Will as normal** - the same toll every other off-turn act already pays (a Parry, an Opportunity Attack, a Riposte). This needs to become a real rule in `magic_overview.md`'s Casting section when the pass happens, not stay a per-spell footnote the way it currently is (half the old spells already say "Off-turn, 1 Tempo Die" verbatim - the pattern exists, it just isn't written as a sanctioned mechanic anywhere yet).
- **No working ever refunds or generates Will, full stop** - Hard Limit 2 wins outright. This has a real casualty: Necration's Mortis Grasp (regain Mana on a kill) and Mortis Harvest (siphon a dying creature's essence into Mana for yourself, an ally, or a banked free Raise Thrall) are both built entirely around this and can't survive as designed - "no working grants an extra cast" (Hard Limit 3) closes the banked-free-cast door too, not just the direct refund. Necration's identity likely needs to lean harder into control/fear/undeath and drop the "kills fuel your engine" angle rather than get a word-swap. **This also resolves a real conflict**: `DESIGN_GUIDE.md`'s own (already-flagged-stale) Healing guideline says "a Necromancer's life-drain should refund Mana, not Wounds" - directly contradicted by this decision. That guideline needs correcting in the same pass, not left to contradict Hard Limit 2 silently.
- **6-8 workings per school, exactly one at Legendary.** Proposed tier template: 2 Lesser / 2-3 Common / 2 Greater / 1 Legendary, Common getting the flex slot since `magic_overview.md` calls it the workhorse tier - not yet locked whether every school uses the same split or it flexes by school (control-heavy schools favoring Common, battle-turning ones favoring Greater). This supersedes the "expand to ~10" number below.

### Per-school notes - which old spells are worth keeping as inspiration, which aren't, and the one open "which is Legendary" choice where more than one candidate qualifies

- **Pyromancy** - already has the one worked Magic Feats example; natural first school to actually convert. Growing Inferno and Ashfall Curtain -> Greater, Immolation -> Common/Sustained, **Incinerate is the clear Legendary**.
- **Aeromancy** - push/pull/reposition throughline. Vortex Snare and Gale Step -> Greater. **Tempest Sovereign (battlefield storm stance) is the clear Legendary.** Zephyr Slip and Gale Barrier are both reactive (see Tempo Die rule above).
- **Geomancy** - **Terra Sepultura (bury and imprison) is the clear Legendary**, a real "takes a turn away" effect. Stoneflesh -> clean Common. Lithic Ruina -> Greater terrain. Earthen Barricade is reactive.
- **Hydromancy** - open choice: Heartfreeze (single-target execute) vs. Glacial Hold (AOE freeze) for Legendary - leaning Glacial Hold since Legendary reads as "alters the scene," demoting Heartfreeze to Greater. Waterlock (punish another caster mid-cast) may not need a current equivalent at all now that every cast already resolves through a resist roll.
- **Shadowmancy** - the outlier at 8 spells, richest kit. Open choice: Void Shroud (banishment, already close to `spells_draft.md`'s own template) vs. Manifest Shadow (living decoy, thematically great but the most mechanically complex thing in any current school) for Legendary - leaning Void Shroud, with Manifest Shadow needing a hard simplification pass regardless of tier. Veil of Shadows (long unbroken invisibility) is a strong Held Will candidate.
- **Benediction** - Chains of Penance -> good Greater. Anathema Brand's "cannot be raised as undead" rider is cheap, purely narrative, survives untouched. **Martyrdom's Price is the likely Legendary** (a whole-fight damage-share pact fits "alters the scene") but needs real simplification - it's currently a two-way damage-redirect.
- **Cultivation** - Temporal Fortification is already shaped almost exactly like the new system wants (temp Dent/Rend buff, Trauma cost on ending) - barely needs touching beyond re-costing. **Respite from Ruin is currently illegal as written** - "remove 1 level of Trauma" violates Hard Limit 2 - needs a real redesign (a Trauma move, Mending-Touch style, not a removal). **No current spell reads as an "alters the scene" Legendary** - this school likely needs a genuinely new capstone (a mass-revival or cheat-death effect fits its life/death-cycle theme), not a promoted old spell.
- **Invocation** - the hardest of the ten; shouldn't be converted alongside the other nine. The Familiar mechanic and the Wound Loss Rule (casting via Wounds instead of Will) are a whole parallel subsystem bolted onto the old Mana model, not individual spells - deciding whether "pay Wounds instead of Will" survives at all has to happen before any spell-by-spell work, not during it. Apotheosis (full transformation) is the thematically obvious Legendary/capstone once that's settled.
- **Necration** - Raise Thrall is Necration's justified signature (`DESIGN_GUIDE.md` reserves summoning almost exclusively for this school). Grave Terror maps cleanly onto a Fear-rating-style effect, matching `spells_draft.md`'s own Instill Fear. Mortis Grasp and Mortis Harvest don't survive as designed - see the no-Will-refund decision above. Legendary is likely a bigger, rarer summon than the Common/Greater Raise Thrall, distinct in scale.
- **Subjugation** - Commanding Whisper (outright compulsion) is currently priced at Common (Mana Cost 3); `magic_overview.md`'s own guard rails say control effects "live only at 9+ and 11+" - needs to move up two tiers, not just get renamed. Gaze of the Sharadhi's three selectable modes need to collapse to one to be the Legendary. Constructed Perception (create anything, illusory) needs the same split-or-simplify treatment.

### Still open

- Whether `DESIGN_GUIDE.md`'s Healing guideline gets corrected in this pass or a dedicated smaller one, now that it's confirmed to conflict with Hard Limit 2.

## Character sheet: three incomplete pieces

- **No Knowledge UI** - no tier picker, no point tracking for the Freebie Pool or Race/Career grants.
- **Priority Allocation doesn't assign, only checks after the fact** - the five priority pickers drive the array/Skill budget/Feat count, but there's no slot-by-slot picker that stops you typing a number the array never handed out.

## Systemic old-DC-scale numbers

Some hardcoded DCs across the book (traps, locks, Wards, monster and NPC abilities) still reflect an older DC scale. Rescale mapping used consistently everywhere it's been touched so far: Very Easy/Easy -> Easy (5); Medium -> Standard (7); Hard/Very Hard -> Hard (9); Grueling/Legendary -> Extreme (11). Already applied throughout `core/equipment/` (`supplies.md`, `alchemy.md`), `core/exploration/` (`traveling.md`, `leadership.md`), and every race/feat file touched by the combat/equipment passes. **Still needs checking** in `general_feats.md`/`martial_feats.md`/`skill_feats.md` once they're rewritten, the per-continent bestiary stubs once they gain content, and the individual spell files during their conversion pass - fold it into each file's own pass rather than running it as a separate global sweep.
