Ward DC by Attribute

Ward = 5 + Attribute; Skill never contributes. What Spell Overcomes and similar effects roll against.

Attributes

BASE = points spent from your Attribute Points budget (separate from Skill Points, no conversion between them). MOD = racial/other modifiers (free, applied after point allocation). TOTAL = BASE + MOD.

Skills

BASE = ranks bought with Skill Points. MOD = free modifiers (career/racial/feat). TOTAL = BASE + MOD, added to 1d12 for that check - Attribute never contributes. BASE can't exceed the governing Attribute's Total; a row that does is flagged in red.

Magic Skills by School

Skill Total = Base + Mod, same as the Skills tab - ARC/FAI never contribute to the roll itself. Your Spell Modifier, used for Spell Attacks vs. Evasion and Spell Overcomes vs. Ward, is this Skill Total minus Armor Penalty.

Spells Known

Each card holds one spell's header fields plus an Effect box for its rules text. Arcane spells need a scribed scroll to cast - track scrolls on hand. Divine spells cast straight from Mana with no prep step; Divine Mana only recovers through Prayer.

Priorities

Assign A through E once each, no repeats. This sets your Level 1 Attribute/Skill Point pools, starting Feat count, highest Career Status tier, and how much Level Adjustment your Race choice covers for free.

Point Budgets

Two separate budgets, no conversion between them. Level 1 size comes from your Priorities above; both then grow with level. There's no separate Skill Cap - a Skill's Rank can never exceed its own governing Attribute's current score, flagged in red on the Skills tab.
