The Exchange

There is no free defense. An attack nobody spends a Tempo Die to Parry simply lands, counting as won by exactly 5 - so the attacker takes an Opening too. A Parry is 1d(Tempo Die) + Weapon Skill + Guard + Edge; higher total wins, ties go to the defender. Per-weapon Attack and Parry numbers are on the Equipment tab; Edge depends on the opponent in front of you, so track it live at the table - see Weapon Length below.

Weapon Length

Every weapon carries a Length, 1-5, printed on it - the squares it can act across at all. A Length mismatch gives the longer weapon's reactionary rolls (a Parry, or an Opportunity Attack) an Edge equal to the raw difference - never your own attack roll - until whoever's shorter lands a hit and takes the Edge for themselves. It depends on the opponent in front of you, so track it live at the table rather than on this sheet.

Attributes

BASE = the standard array your Attributes priority handed you, one number per Attribute, plus the +1 granted at levels 4/8/12. MOD = racial/other modifiers (free, applied on top of the array and not limited by it - a negative TOTAL is legal). TOTAL = BASE + MOD.

Skills

BASE = ranks bought with Skill Points. MOD = free modifiers (career/racial/feat). TOTAL = BASE + MOD, added to 1d12 for that check - Attribute never contributes. BASE can't exceed the governing Attribute's Total; a row that does is flagged in red.

Armor

Check "Worn" for equipped armor - only worn pieces contribute to AR / Armor Penalty above. AR is Durability: starts equal to Max AR, drops by 1 each hit (2 from a War Maul or any firearm); Max AR is the repair cap. Rigid armor loses no durability to a blow it absorbs entirely and cannot be field-repaired once Broken; Flexible loses 1 to every hit and can always be patched back. Penalty is derived from Max AR, not tracked per piece.

Shields

Guard is passive - it adds to every Parry you make while the shield is Equipped, whatever you actually parry with, and needs no Skill. Only the highest equipped shield's Guard counts; it never stacks. It degrades by 1 whenever a Parry it added to is lost, and is restored with your armor by the same Crafting repair. A Chain Flail (Ignores Guard) sets it to 0 for as long as it is the thing swinging at you. The shield is also a Length 1 implement in its own right: it Parries at any range using Daggers & Wrestling, though it never carries an Edge of its own, being as short as anything gets.

Magic Skills

Skill Total = Base + Mod, same as the Skills tab - ARC/FAI never contribute to the roll itself. Arcane and Divine resolve very differently: Arcane funds a per-school Spell Modifier used for Spell Attacks/Overcomes; Divine's Petition Roll is always flat 1d12 vs. DC 7 with no modifier at all - a Divine school's Skill Total instead sets Rite Mastery, reroll charges per Long Rest.

A Spell Attack is answered by nothing - no Tempo Die, no Parry, no Ward. Roll 1d12 + Spell Modifier against the Shot DC, the same number a bowshot answers to, set by range, movement and position rather than by the target. A Spell Overcome instead rolls against the target's Ward (Overview tab); Divine's Petition Roll is flat 1d12 vs. DC 7. Nothing here criticals - there are no critical hits in Ressam.

Spells Known

Each card holds one spell's header fields plus an Effect box for its rules text. Arcane spells need a scribed scroll to cast - track scrolls on hand. Divine spells cast straight from Mana with no prep step; Divine Mana only recovers through Prayer.

Priorities

Assign A through E once each, no repeats. This sets your Level 1 Attribute/Skill Point pools, starting Feat count, highest Career Status tier, and which Race Tier(s) you may pick from.

Point Budgets

Two separate budgets, no conversion between them. Level 1 size comes from your Priorities above; both then grow with level. There's no separate Skill Cap - a Skill's Rank can never exceed its own governing Attribute's current score, flagged in red on the Skills tab.
